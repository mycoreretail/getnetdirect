Report definitions & data limits

Received counts use original lead creation dates. First signup and first completion charts use recorded event timestamps, not the last edit date. Undated historical signups/completions are not assigned invented dates. A later cancellation does not erase a genuine past signup/completion event; current-outcome tables show the result today. Void records are excluded from all charts and conversion denominators.

Historical activity logging begins with v11. Displayed contact or verification ages for older records without an event timestamp are conservative flags based on their received date. Payouts remain in the separate Admin ledger; these counts are not revenue, profit or carrier commission reports.
