Recommended Operating Rules

These rules keep statuses and partner payouts consistent when you add more sales reps and referral sources.

Use Void for bad records

Duplicates, test leads, fake submissions and records entered by mistake should be Void — not Cancelled.

Use Cancelled after an order

Use Cancelled when a customer started or completed an order and later cancelled it.

Use Not Interested separately

This makes your conversion reporting more accurate than grouping every lost customer under Cancelled.

Approve payout after eligibility

Keep partner payout Pending until your payout rule is met, then Approved, then Paid with date and payment reference.

Set a next follow-up date

Every open lead should have a next action date so leads do not disappear in the dashboard.

One account per person/business

Each employee, contractor rep, leasing office or realtor should have their own account so attribution stays clean.
