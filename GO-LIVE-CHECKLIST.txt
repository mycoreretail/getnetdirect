Recommended operating rules:
1. Void = duplicate, test, fake or invalid lead.
2. Cancelled = customer had an order/process underway and later cancelled.
3. Not Interested = customer declined the offer.
4. Not Serviceable = no supported provider/service was available.
5. Complete = installation/order is fully complete.
6. Keep a next follow-up date on every open lead.
7. Keep partner payout Pending until your eligibility rule is met.
8. Mark Paid only after payment is actually sent and enter the payment reference.

IMPORTANT BEFORE MULTI-USER GO-LIVE:
The current GitHub Pages version uses browser localStorage. Data created or changed on one device does not automatically sync to another device. This is fine for testing the interface, but a shared database/backend is required for real employees, contractors and referral partners to work from separate phones/computers.

Recommended live architecture:
GetNetDirect.com public site -> shared API/database -> Admin Portal / Sales Rep Portal / Partner Portal

Do not collect Social Security numbers, full card information or carrier passwords in these lead forms.
