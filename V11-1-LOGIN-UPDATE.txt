GETNETDIRECT v11.1 — MY LOGIN UPDATE

New Admin feature:
- Open Admin Portal -> My Login.
- Enter your current password.
- Change your username, password, or both.
- New password must be at least 8 characters.
- Current password is required for every credential change.
- Username changes are checked for duplicates.
- Your current session is refreshed automatically after a successful change.
